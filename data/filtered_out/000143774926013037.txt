 Item&#160;7.01

 Regulation FD Disclosure

&#160;

Press Releases

&#160;

On April 22, 2026, CervoMed Inc. (the &#8220;Company,&#8221; &#8220;we&#8221; or &#8220;us&#8221;) issued a press release announcing data from the Company&#8217;s Phase 2b RewinD-LB trial presented at the 2026 American Academy of Neurology (&#8220;AAN&#8221;) Annual Meeting that demonstrated neflamapimod increased basal forebrain volume and functional connectivity in patients with dementia with Lewy bodies. A copy of the press release is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 and incorporated herein by reference.

&#160;
