April 15, 2026 WASHINGTON – Yesterday, Secretary of the Treasury Scott Bessent met with Saudi Minister of Finance Mohammed bin Abdullah al-Jadaan to discuss ongoing economic cooperation between the United States and Saudi Arabia. Secretary Bessent and Minister al-Jadaan highlighted progress on several bilateral initiatives being implemented by the U.S. and Saudi Arabia. During their meeting, Secretary Bessent was pleased to join with Minister al-Jadaan in signing a Tax Information Exchange Agreement.
