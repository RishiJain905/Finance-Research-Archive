Item
4.02 Non-Reliance on Previously Issued Financial Statements or a Related Audit Report or Completed Interim Review. 

&#160;

On
April 13, 2026, management of Nature&#8217;s Miracle Holdings, Inc. (the &#8220;Company&#8221;) determined that disclosure was necessary
and that action should be taken to preclude any further reliance on the Company&#8217;s previously issued unaudited condensed consolidated
financial statements and related disclosures as of and for the period ended September 30, 2025. These financial statements were included
in the Company&#8217;s Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission (the &#8220;SEC&#8221;) on November
14, 2025 (the &#8220;Original Filing&#8221;), and the determination was made to correctly record an amount in short term loans erroneously
omitted from the Original Filing.

&#160;

More
specifically, the Company has restated its condensed financial statements as of and for the three and nine months ended September 30,
2025, to appropriately include the recording of the short term loan.

&#160;

The
Company will file with the SEC an amendment to the Original Filing via a Form 10-Q/A for the quarterly period ended September 30, 2025.

&#160;

The
Company&#8217;s management and the Audit Committee have discussed the matters disclosed in this Current Report on Form 8-K pursuant to
this Item 4.02 with the Company&#8217;s independent registered public accounting firm, as well as with WWC, P.C., the Company&#8217;s
auditor for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025.
