Item
5.03 Amendments to Articles of Incorporation or Bylaws; Change in Fiscal Year.

&#160;

On
April 23, 2026, M2i Global, Inc. (the &#8220;Company&#8221;) filed with the Nevada Secretary of State a Certificate of Amendment to Designation
of Series B Preferred Stock (the &#8220;Certificate of Amendment&#8221;).

&#160;

The
Certificate of Amendment amends the Company&#8217;s previously designated Series B Preferred Stock to increase the number of authorized
shares of Series B Preferred Stock from 500 shares to 750 shares.

&#160;

The
amendment was approved in accordance with Nevada law and the Company&#8217;s governing documents.

&#160;

The
foregoing description of the Certificate of Amendment does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to
the full text of the Certificate of Amendment, a copy of which is filed as Exhibit 3.1 hereto and incorporated herein by reference.
